# QUAF SEASON 09 — GROUP LEADER GUIDE

This document instructs Group Leaders on managing their group delegates, submitting registrations, and monitoring live progress.

---

## 1. Access & Group Identification

- **URL**: `/leader`
- **Role**: Authenticated Group Leader.
- **Group Brand Identity**:
  - Each Leader's portal is dynamically branded with their Group's official color and emblem:
    - **Pacto Hikmic**: `#2e3192` (Royal Blue)
    - **Yugo Rushdic**: `#ad1e56` (Berry Magenta)
    - **Conco Majdic**: `#f8e709` (Golden Yellow)
    - **Lumo Fikric**: `#56286b` (Violet Purple)
    - **Unio Hilmic**: `#7f1518` (Crimson Maroon)

---

## 2. Registration Rules & Quotas for Leaders

### Individual Registrations
- Each student is capped at **maximum 5 individual programmes** (own zone + eligible Mix zone).
- A 6th registration attempt will be blocked automatically by the system.
- Once submitted, individual entries are marked as `pending` until verified by the Admin Desk.

### Group Programme Registrations
- Access **Group Registrations** tab (`/leader/registrations/group`).
- Select the group competition (e.g. Choir, Group Recitation).
- Select participating delegates from your group within the allowed participant range (`min` to `max`).
- Group registrations **do not reduce** participating students' individual 5-programme quota.

---

## 3. Real-Time Tracking & Schedules

- **Dashboard**: Track total confirmed registrations, upcoming events, and live group scores.
- **Schedule Conflict Warnings**: When registering a student for overlapping events, the system warns the leader with conflict details so alternate allocations can be made.
- **Results & Standings**: Live festival results and position updates are updated automatically upon declaration.
