# QUAF SEASON 09 — JUDGE EVALUATION GUIDE

This document guides stage and off-stage judges on marking, criteria, and score sheet submission for **QUAF Season 09**.

---

## 1. Access & Authentication

- **URL**: `/judge`
- **Authentication**: Sign in using the designated Judge account.
- **Assigned Programmes**: The judge dashboard lists all programmes allocated to the judge.

---

## 2. Evaluation Workflow

1. **Select Active Competition**:
   - Click on the assigned programme currently marked `in_progress`.
2. **Review Participants**:
   - Participants are presented with their secret Chest Numbers (assigned via Green Room).
   - Names and Groups are blinded where applicable to maintain evaluation integrity.
3. **Scoring Criteria (0–100 Scale)**:
   - Evaluated on criteria established for the programme (e.g. Pronunciation, Content, Expression, Presentation).
   - Total score translates directly to official Festival Grades:
     - **90–100**: Grade **A+** (6 points)
     - **70–89**: Grade **A** (5 points)
     - **60–69**: Grade **B** (3 points)
     - **50–59**: Grade **C** (1 point)
     - **Below 50**: No grade points
4. **Submit Score Sheet**:
   - Review entered marks.
   - Click **Submit Score Sheet** to lock marks and transfer them to the Tabulation Desk.
   - Once submitted, scores cannot be modified without Admin override.
