# QUAF SEASON 09 — ZONE & GROUP RULES

This document defines the official **Zones** and **Groups**, their terminology constraints, brand colors, and structural mappings.

---

## 1. Strict Terminology Rule

The following legacy terms are **strictly prohibited** in QUAF Season 09:
- ❌ **House**
- ❌ **Team**
- ❌ **Category** (when referring to Zones or Groups)

### Authorized Terminology:
- **GROUP**: The primary competition unit / delegate collective.
- **ZONE**: The division of students and programmes (A Zone, B Zone, C Zone, Mix Zone).
- **CATEGORY / DISCIPLINE**: Subject area (Quran, Literature, Arts, General).

---

## 3. The 4 Official Zones

Every student and programme belongs to an official Zone:

| Zone Code | Official Name | Target Classes | Description |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| `A_ZONE` | **A Zone** | Class 1 to 4 | Primary junior competitions |
| `B_ZONE` | **B Zone** | Class 5 to 7 | Intermediate competitions |
| `C_ZONE` | **C Zone** | Class 8 to 10 | Senior competitions |
| `MIX_ZONE` | **Mix Zone** | General / Combined | Open competitions across zones |

### Cross-Zone Rules:
- A student from **A Zone** can ONLY participate in **A Zone** programmes and eligible **Mix Zone** programmes.
- A student from **B Zone** can ONLY participate in **B Zone** programmes and eligible **Mix Zone** programmes.
- A student from **C Zone** can ONLY participate in **C Zone** programmes and eligible **Mix Zone** programmes.
- Cross-zone participation (e.g. A Zone student entering B Zone programme) is strictly prohibited and blocked by the eligibility engine.
